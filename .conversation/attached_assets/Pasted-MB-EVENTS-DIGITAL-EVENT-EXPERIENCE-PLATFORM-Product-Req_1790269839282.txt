MB EVENTS — DIGITAL EVENT EXPERIENCE PLATFORM

Product Requirements Document (PRD)

1. PRODUCT VISION

MB Events should not feel like another DJ website.

We are building a complete digital event platform around the existing entertainment business.

The customer should be able to go from:

“I’m thinking about having a party.”

to:

Planning → Venue → Vendors → Invitation → RSVPs → Guest Management → Entertainment → Event Day → Photos → Reviews → Referrals

without leaving the MB ecosystem.

The goal is to make customers feel that when they hire MB, they are not simply hiring a DJ.

They are getting an event concierge + planning platform + entertainment company.
